# Ingredients
- 200g [[Tempeh|tempeh]]
- [[Light Soy Sauce|Light soy sauce]]
- [[Paprika]]
- [[Cumin]]
- 1 tsp [[Liquid Smoke]]
- [[Black Pepper|Black pepper]]
- ½ [[Yellow Onion|onion]], sliced into half-rings
- 2 cloves [[Garlic|garlic]], minced
- ½ a [[Bell Pepper|bell pepper]], thinly sliced
- [[Spinach|Spinach]] or [[Rocket|rocket]] or [[Lettuce|lettuce]]
- ¼ a [[Cucumber|cucumber]]
- 1 [[Tomato|tomato]]
- [[Sesame Oil|Sesame oil]]

# Directions
1. Cut the tempeh into ±16 slices.
2. Make a mix of a dash of soy sauce, some paprika powder, black pepper and cumin, and add 1 teaspoon of liquid smoke.
3. Cover the tempeh in the mix, and add a generous amount of sesame oil to the pan.
4. Fry the tempeh for 6-8 minutes on each side, though rather too short than too long.
5. Cut the onion in half-rings, and chop the garlic, fruit until glassy.
6. Fry the bell pepper for 2-3 minutes until softened.
