# Ingredients
## Beef Filling
- 500g [[Minced Meat|minced meat]]
- 1 tsp [[Garlic Powder|garlic powder]]
- 1 tsp [[Onion Powder|onion powder]]
- 3 tsp [[Worcestershire Sauce|Worcestershire sauce]]
- ⅔ tsp [[Salt|salt]]
- ¼ tsp [[Black Pepper|pepper]]
## Sauce
- 2-3 tbsp [[Greek Yogurt|Greek yogurt]]
- 1.5 tbsp [[Mayonnaise|mayonnaise]]
- 1 tbsp [[Ketchup|ketchup]]
- 1 tsp [[Dijon Mustard|dijon mustard]]
- ¼ tsp [[Garlic Powder|garlic powder]]
- Pinch of [[Paprika|paprika]]
## Serving
- 4 [[Tortilla|tortillas]], large
- 1 head of [[Lettuce|lettuce]]
- ½ cup [[Cheddar Cheese|cheddar cheese]], shredded
- *⅓ cup [[Yellow Onion|onion]], chopped*

# Directions
1. Brown the beef with garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper. Add the Worcestershire sauce *and onions*, and cook until nicely browned and a little crispy around the edges. Set aside.
2. Mix all the sauce ingredients in a bowl. Taste and adjust.
3. Spread sauce on each wrap, pile on beef, lettuce, cheese, and onions. Roll up like a burrito.
4. *Toast the wrap in a skillet for 1-2 minutes each side for a crunchy wrap.*
