# Ingredients
## Broth
- 1 pack [[Silken Tofu|silken tofu]]
- 300-350mL [[Soy Milk|unsweetened soy milk]]
- 1 tbsp [[Tahini|tahini]]
- 1 tbsp [[Peanut Butter|peanut butter]]
- 1½ tsp [[Light Soy Sauce|light soy sauce]]
- ½ tsp [[Rice Vinegar|rice vinegar]]
- 1 tsp [[Maple Syrup|maple syrup]] or [[Agave|agave]]
- 2 tsp [[Sesame Oil|sesame oil]]
- Pinch of [[Salt|salt]]
- Ice cubes
## Others
- 2-3 bundles [[Noodles|noodles]] of choice
- *[[Kimchi]]*
- *[[Cucumber|Cucumber]]*
- *Toasted [[Sesame Seed|sesame seeds]]*
- *Crispy [[Tofu|tofu]]/[[Tempeh|tempeh]]*
# Notes
- 2 servings
- Requirements: Blender
- Time: 
# Directions
1. Blend all of the broth ingredients together with a few ice cubes until completely smooth and creamy.
2. Cook your noodles according to packet instructions, then rinse really well under cold water until chilled.
3. Divide the noodles between bowls and pour over the cold broth.
4. Top however you like. All of the *italic* options are recommended toppings; some extra ice cubes to keep everything cold are also recommended.
