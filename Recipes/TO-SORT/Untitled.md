# Ingredients
- 3 cups [[Cranberry Juice|Cranberry juice]]
- ⅓ cup [[Frozen Orange Juice Concentrate|frozen orange juice concentrate]], thawed
- ½ cup [[Frozen Pineapple Juice Concentrate|frozen pineapple juice concentrate]], thawed
- 3 tbsp [[Lemon Juice|lemon juice]]
- 1 cup [[Brandy|brandy]]
- 1 cup ice cubes
- 750mL [[Prosecco|prosecco]], cold
- 1 [[Orange|orange]], scrubbed and thinly sliced
# Notes
- X servings
- Requirements:
- Contains XX% ABV
# Directions
1. In a large bowl or pitcher, combine the cranberry juice, orange juice concentrate, pineapple juice concentrate, lemon juice, and brandy, and stir to blend.
2. When ready to serve, add the ice to the mix and stir to chill and blend. Add the prosecco and stir gently. Fill each glass with ice, pour in the punch, and garnish with an orange slice.
